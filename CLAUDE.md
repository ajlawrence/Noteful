# Project Memory — "AI in a Box" Offer One-Pager

> This branch (`claude/ai-box-offer-refine-u2jcyb`) is being used to draft and
> refine a marketing **one-pager**, not to change the Noteful Rails app. This
> file is the single source of truth so we stop drifting between drafts.

## What the deliverable is

A standalone **one-pager** describing a productized service: building an
**agentic marketing pipeline** for a client's single highest-leverage channel.

- It lives inside an email body **but is not the email**. The user writes the
  personal note that wraps it.
- It must **survive forwarding** — read the same to a stranger as to the
  original recipient. No greeting, no subject line, no sign-off, no
  personalization tokens.

## Locked decisions

- **One box, not multiple.** Channel **discovery is baked in** (the agentic +
  fractional-CMO analysis that picks the channel), never sold separately.
- **Price:** $3,000 flat. **~2 weeks.** No price range, no retainer/hourly in
  the one-pager.
- **Avatar:** people who've built a real product, strong engineering, already
  using AI on the build side. **Do NOT label them** ("founders", "technical",
  "autistic", etc.). Select them by *what they've built*, not a tag.
- **Lead with "what this is"** in the first 2–3 sentences, spoken to the
  avatar — then move into "I work with people…".

## Voice rules (hard-won — violating these is the main failure mode)

- Peer-to-peer, plain, dry, a little self-deprecating. **Not marketing copy.**
- **No declarative statements about the reader** ("you've built X", "marketing
  never got built that way"). Use **conditional / "feel" framing** instead
  ("if you're like most of the people I work with, marketing doesn't feel
  like it fits…").
- **Do not** say marketing "gets handed off." Marketing is many things, done
  in pieces, internal and external — the point is *the resource never gets
  understood*, not that they outsourced it.
- Kill AI-slop tells: "honest", "actually", "that X matters", rhythmic
  triads, staged one-line reveals, scare-quote winks, fake intimacy.
- **No sales/justification posture.** Posture is: I'd like to help; if it
  fits, let's work together; if not, fine — tell me if I can be useful.

## Core framing / message

- Marketing, sales, revenue **can** be structured, project-managed, and run on
  logic like everything else they've built. The problem is **how it's
  treated** — the conversation around it keeps it vague and
  personality-driven.
- Differentiator: AI makes the *generating/thinking* cheap; the value is the
  **harness + taste/judgment** from ~30 years. The AI does the volume; knowing
  what good looks like points it at the right thing.

## Why he's doing it (his words, not invented)

Not building another agency or program. Wants to work with people doing
interesting things to keep exploring what's possible with this and get better
at it; work tends to come via referral (Daniel and people like him). Boy Scout
rule: leave it better than I found it.

## Current draft

```
If you're like most of the people I work with, marketing doesn't feel like it
fits the rest of what you've built — it never quite works as well as the other
parts of the business, and it's harder to see into. What I build is that
missing piece: a working marketing pipeline for your highest-leverage channel
(which one is on me to figure out), run by AI but built around your company —
your customers, your positioning, your voice. About two weeks, $3,000.

I work with people who've already got the product and use AI across the
engineering, but never aimed it here. Marketing tends to get done in pieces —
some external, some internal — and the resource itself never really gets
understood. It doesn't have to be that way. Marketing, sales and revenue can be
structured and run on logic like anything else you build; they just rarely get
treated that way, because the conversation around marketing keeps it vague and
personality-driven.

I built and sold a few marketing firms — the last one over ten years ago. Since
then I've been a fractional CMO and advisor to PE- and VC-backed companies, and
I sit on the board of a public one. I've run my own businesses too, close to
eight figures. Thirty years of that is what the AI runs on top of; it's just
what finally lets me do the work in a couple of weeks instead of with a team
over months.

I'm not building another agency or a program. I take this on with people doing
interesting things — it's how I keep learning what's possible and get better at
it, and the work tends to come from whoever the last one sent my way. I leave
it better than I found it.

If it's something you want to take on together, let's do it. If not, no problem
— but if I can be useful, tell me.
```
